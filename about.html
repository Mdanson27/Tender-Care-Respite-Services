About Tender Care

Care rooted in compassion, dignity and family partnership.

Tender Care Respite Services is committed to safe, inclusive and dependable support that gives children meaningful experiences and families room to rest, recharge and feel supported.

Our purpose

Support that respects the whole child and the whole family.

Care is shaped around each child’s routines, interests, strengths, abilities and family expectations, with clear communication and teamwork.

Founder: Juliet N.

Business statement

Safe, compassionate, inclusive and dependable respite care.

We aim to give children meaningful experiences while giving families and caregivers the opportunity to rest, recharge and feel supported.

Mission

Compassionate care that supports well-being.

To provide compassionate, safe, and reliable respite care that supports children and families while promoting each child’s dignity, independence, development, and well-being.

Vision

A trusted partner for children and families.

To become a trusted respite care provider known for creating a safe, nurturing, inclusive, and supportive environment where every child can grow, explore, and feel valued while families receive the support and peace of mind they deserve.

Core values

The principles behind every interaction.

Compassion

Kindness, patience and understanding.

Safety

Physical and emotional safety first.

Respect

Respect for every child, family, culture and ability.

Inclusion

Every child deserves to feel welcomed and valued.

Integrity

Honest, dependable and professional service.

Dignity

Recognition of strengths, choices and individuality.

Partnership

Working together with families and caregivers.

Our goals

What Tender Care works toward.

  • Provide safe, quality care tailored to each child.
  • Give families dependable respite support they can trust.
  • Promote development through play, learning and social interaction.
  • Create an inclusive environment where every child feels valued.
  • Build strong family relationships through open communication.
  • Encourage independence and confidence through age-appropriate activities.
  • Provide flexible and reliable services that respond to family needs.
