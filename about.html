About Tender Care

Compassionate care, built around dignity and belonging.

Tender Care Respite Services exists to support children with respect and individuality while giving parents and caregivers dependable space to rest, work, attend appointments and recharge.

TC
Our purpose

We believe respite care should support the child and the family at the same time.

Care is more meaningful when it respects familiar routines, recognizes individual strengths and listens to the people who know the child best. Tender Care works from that starting point.

Our goal is to create a safe, nurturing and inclusive environment where children can participate in meaningful activities while families feel supported, informed and respected.

Founder: Juliet N.
Mission

Support children. Strengthen families. Protect dignity.

To provide compassionate, safe, and reliable respite care that supports children and families while promoting each child’s dignity, independence, development, and well-being.

Vision

A trusted care partner for brighter family days.

To become a trusted respite care provider known for creating a safe, nurturing, inclusive, and supportive environment where every child can grow, explore, and feel valued while families receive the support and peace of mind they deserve.

Core values

The principles that guide how care should feel.

These values shape the way Tender Care approaches children, families, communication and responsibility.

Compassion

Kindness, patience and understanding in every interaction.

Safety

Children’s physical and emotional safety remain a central priority.

Respect

Respect for each child, family, culture, ability and individual difference.

Inclusion

Every child deserves to feel welcomed, valued and included.

Integrity

Honest, dependable, responsible and professional conduct.

Dignity

Recognition of strengths, choices, individuality and personal worth.

Partnership

Working with families and caregivers to support consistency and trust.

Business statement

Meaningful experiences for children. Practical support for caregivers.

Tender Care Respite Services is committed to providing safe, compassionate, inclusive and dependable respite care that gives children meaningful experiences while giving families and caregivers the opportunity to rest, recharge and feel supported.

What this looks like
  • Listening carefully before care begins.
  • Respecting routines, interests, abilities and family expectations.
  • Creating age-appropriate opportunities for play and participation.
  • Keeping parents and caregivers informed.
  • Being clear when a requested service requires additional qualifications or safeguards.
