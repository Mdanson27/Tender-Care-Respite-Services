Our services

Flexible, individualized support for children and caregivers.

Services are considered according to each family’s needs, staff availability and applicable Alberta requirements.

01

Respite Care

Short-term care giving parents and caregivers time to rest, attend appointments, work, manage responsibilities or recharge.

02

In-Home Child Support

Care and supervision for children in the comfort and familiarity of their home environment.

03

Evening & Weekend Support

Flexible care options for families requiring support outside regular daytime hours.

04

After-School Support

Supervision, play, routines and age-appropriate activities after school.

05

School Break & Holiday Support

Childcare and recreational support during school holidays and breaks.

06

Play & Recreation

Arts and crafts, sensory play, outdoor activities, reading, music and structured or unstructured play.

07

Community Support

Support for appropriate community and recreational activities when agreed upon with the family.

08

Individualized Support

Care plans shaped around the child’s interests, routines, strengths, abilities and family expectations.

09

Family Communication

Regular communication with parents and caregivers about activities, experiences and important information.

Individualized care

The exact support can look different for every family.

Tender Care can discuss short-term childcare, support with daily routines, social and community activities, individualized care and caregiver relief.

Service scope

Specialized services require appropriate safeguards.

Personal care, transportation, medication, complex medical needs or other specialized supports should only be offered when the appropriate training, insurance, permissions, licensing and regulatory requirements are in place.

Please discuss these needs before booking so suitability can be considered.
