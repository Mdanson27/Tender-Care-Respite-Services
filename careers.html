Careers at Tender Care

Join a caring team creating brighter days.

We are looking for compassionate, reliable, dedicated and qualified individuals who share our commitment to safe, respectful and person-centred care.

Join our caring team

Make a meaningful difference in the lives of children, adults and families.

At Tender Care Respite Services, we are passionate about supporting people living with disabilities and the families who care for them.

Our team is built around compassion, professionalism, safety, respect and dependable service. We are interested in people who bring both the right qualifications and the right attitude to care.

Career opportunities

Roles we welcome applications for.

Position availability may vary. Applicants should be prepared to meet role-specific screening, training and service requirements.

01

Respite Support Workers

Provide dependable short-term support that gives families and primary caregivers time to rest, work, attend appointments or recharge.

02

Child & Youth Care Workers

Support children and young people through safe supervision, routines, recreation, learning and age-appropriate participation.

03

Disability Support Workers

Promote dignity, independence, inclusion and meaningful participation for individuals living with disabilities.

04

Health Care Aides

Provide support within appropriate training, scope, permissions and care requirements while protecting safety and dignity.

05

Community Support Workers

Help individuals participate in suitable community, recreational and social activities based on agreed support plans.

06

Behaviour Support Workers

Support person-centred routines and behaviour strategies within the worker’s qualifications and approved support plan.

Qualifications & requirements

What we look for in applicants.

  • Experience supporting children or adults with disabilities.
  • Valid First Aid and CPR certification.
  • Clear Vulnerable Sector and Criminal Record Check.
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills.
  • Compassion, patience, professionalism and reliability.
  • Valid driver’s licence and reliable transportation are assets.
