Clear expectations for families

Policies designed to support reliable scheduling and respectful communication.

Tender Care understands that family circumstances can change unexpectedly. These guidelines help families know what to expect before, during and after scheduled care.

Scheduling & cancellations

Simple expectations make care easier to coordinate.

Families are encouraged to provide as much notice as possible whenever a scheduled service needs to change.

01

Scheduled Care

Appointments and respite shifts are scheduled in advance and depend on staff availability.

02

24-Hour Notice

Families should provide at least 24 hours’ notice whenever possible when cancelling a scheduled service.

03

Late Cancellation

Cancellations made with less than 24 hours’ notice may be subject to a cancellation fee as outlined in the family’s service agreement.

04

Emergency Cancellation

Emergency situations will be considered individually and handled with compassion and reasonable flexibility.

05

Late Arrival

Families are asked to communicate if they will be late for a scheduled pickup or handover.

06

Schedule Changes

Requests for additional hours or changes to scheduled care depend on availability and must be confirmed by Tender Care Respite Services.

Why communication matters

Changes are easier to manage when everyone knows what is happening.

Clear notice allows Tender Care to plan responsibly, protect staff availability and keep expectations transparent for families.

Notify early

Provide as much notice as possible for changes or cancellations.

Confirm changes

Additional hours or schedule adjustments are not final until confirmed.

Communicate delays

Let Tender Care know if a pickup or handover will be late.

Discuss emergencies

Unexpected situations are considered individually with reasonable flexibility.

Service scope

Some types of support require additional qualifications and safeguards.

Tender Care’s exact legal service scope depends on its Alberta business setup, applicable licensing or regulatory obligations, staff training, insurance, permissions and the needs of the child.

Personal care

Personal care should only be offered when appropriate training, permissions, insurance and any applicable regulatory requirements are in place.

Transportation

Transportation should only be provided when suitable authorization, insurance, safety arrangements and any applicable requirements are in place.

Medication

Medication-related support should only be offered when the appropriate training, permissions, procedures and safeguards are in place.

Complex medical needs

Support involving complex medical needs should only be offered where Tender Care is appropriately trained, equipped, insured and permitted to provide the requested care.

Specialized support

Any specialized support should be discussed before booking so suitability, qualifications and required safeguards can be considered.

Family service agreement

The specific arrangement should be confirmed before care begins.

A family service agreement can outline the details that apply to that family, including scheduling, fees, responsibilities, expectations and any agreed care arrangements.

Important note

Website information does not replace family-specific agreements.

Where a family service agreement contains more specific terms, those terms should be reviewed carefully and discussed directly with Tender Care.

Need clarification before booking? Call Tender Care at 780-616-2248 to discuss scheduling, cancellations, service boundaries or any support needs that may require additional training or permissions.
