Clear expectations

Scheduling and cancellation information for families.

Tender Care understands that family circumstances can change unexpectedly. These guidelines support clear, respectful scheduling.

01

Scheduled Care

Appointments and respite shifts are scheduled in advance and depend on staff availability.

02

Cancellation

Families should provide at least 24 hours’ notice whenever possible. Cancellations with less than 24 hours’ notice may be subject to a cancellation fee as outlined in the family’s service agreement.

03

Emergency Cancellation

Emergencies happen. Emergency situations will be considered individually and handled with compassion and reasonable flexibility.

04

Late Arrival

Families are asked to communicate if they will be late for a scheduled pickup or handover.

05

Schedule Changes

Requests for additional hours or changes to scheduled care depend on availability and must be confirmed by Tender Care Respite Services.

06

Communication

Families are encouraged to provide as much notice as possible when cancelling or changing a scheduled service.

Service scope

Care should match training, permissions and requirements.

The exact services Tender Care can provide may depend on its Alberta business setup, applicable licensing or regulatory obligations, staff qualifications, insurance and the needs of the child.

Important

Please discuss specialized support before booking.

Personal care, transportation, medication, support involving complex medical needs or other specialized services should only be offered when appropriate training, insurance, permissions and regulatory requirements are in place.

Tender Care can discuss the requested support and whether it is suitable to provide.

Family service agreements: Specific booking terms, fees, responsibilities and care arrangements should be confirmed in the applicable family service agreement.
Questions?

Clear communication comes first.

Call Tender Care before booking if you need clarification about scheduling, cancellations or service boundaries.
