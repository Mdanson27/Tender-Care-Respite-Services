Professional team structure

Clear leadership. Coordinated care. Accountable support.

Tender Care Respite Services is building a professional structure designed to keep care organized, responsive, safe and centred on the people and families we serve.

Tender Care, Brighter Days

A team structure designed around service quality and family trust.

Strong respite care depends on more than frontline support. It also requires leadership, coordination, human resources, quality assurance and access to specialist guidance.

The structure below shows how Tender Care intends to organize accountability and support across the agency as the team grows.

Organizational chart

How the professional team is structured.

Leadership sets direction, program management coordinates service delivery, and specialist roles strengthen quality, safety and workforce support.

Executive leadership

Founder & Executive Director

Juliet N.

Agency leadership

Operations & Program Manager

Service management

Care Coordinator

Supports intake, care planning, scheduling, family communication and continuity between families and support workers.

Workforce support

Human Resources & Administration

Supports recruitment, screening, onboarding, staff records, administration and workplace processes.

Specialist guidance

Clinical / Behaviour Consultant

Provides specialist guidance within professional scope for behavioural, developmental or clinical support needs.

Quality assurance

Quality & Safety Coordinator

Supports policies, incident learning, documentation standards, service quality and safety improvement.

How the team works
