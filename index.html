A thoughtful approach to respite

Support should feel personal, calm and dependable from the very first conversation.

Tender Care Respite Services is designed to give children meaningful, age-appropriate support while helping parents and primary caregivers create time to rest, work, attend appointments, manage responsibilities or simply recharge.

Our approach begins by understanding the child: what they enjoy, what helps them feel safe, how they communicate, the routines that matter to them and what the family expects from care.

Meaningful time in care

Activities can be simple, familiar and purposeful.

Depending on the child, family expectations and the agreed care plan, support may include reading, music, arts and crafts, sensory play, outdoor activity, structured play, unstructured play and appropriate community participation.

The goal is not to fill every minute. It is to help the child feel safe, engaged, respected and comfortable.

Family communication

Families should stay connected to the care experience.

Regular communication can include relevant information about activities, routines, experiences and anything important that the parent or caregiver should know.

Open communication also helps care remain aligned with changing family needs and expectations.

Helpful information

Questions families often need answered before scheduling.

For family-specific questions, call Tender Care directly so the requested support can be discussed properly.

Call 780-616-2248
How far in advance should care be scheduled?

Appointments and respite shifts are scheduled in advance and depend on staff availability. Earlier communication is encouraged whenever possible.

What is the cancellation expectation?

Families are asked to provide at least 24 hours’ notice whenever possible. Less than 24 hours’ notice may be subject to a cancellation fee as outlined in the family service agreement.

Can care be arranged for evenings or weekends?

Evening and weekend support is among the service options and is arranged according to availability.

Can Tender Care support additional developmental, social or physical needs?

This can be discussed with the family. Support should only be provided where Tender Care is appropriately equipped, qualified and permitted to provide the requested service.

What about medication, transportation or complex medical needs?

These services should only be offered when the appropriate training, insurance, permissions and regulatory requirements are in place. Please discuss specialized needs before booking.

Responsible service scope
