Inclusive support

Every child deserves to feel safe, respected and included.

Tender Care welcomes families from diverse backgrounds and can discuss support for children who benefit from individualized attention, structured activities or additional developmental, social or physical support where the service is appropriately equipped and qualified.

  • Support built around strengths, routines and preferences.
  • Age-appropriate participation and meaningful activities.
  • Clear communication with parents and caregivers.
  • Respect for culture, ability and individual difference.

Helpful information

Questions families often ask before scheduling.

For family-specific questions, contact Tender Care directly.

Can care be arranged for evenings or weekends?

Evening and weekend support is among the service options and is arranged according to availability.

How much cancellation notice is requested?

Families are asked to provide at least 24 hours’ notice whenever possible. Less notice may be subject to a fee as outlined in the family service agreement.

Can Tender Care support additional developmental, social or physical needs?

This can be discussed with the family. Support should only be provided where Tender Care is appropriately equipped, qualified and permitted to provide the requested service.

What about medication, transportation or complex medical needs?

These services should only be offered when the appropriate training, insurance, permissions and regulatory requirements are in place.
