Who we serve

Respectful support for children and the people who care for them.

Tender Care welcomes families from diverse backgrounds and is committed to compassionate, dependable and inclusive care.

Families we support

Respite that responds to real needs.

  • Families seeking occasional or regular respite care.
  • Parents and primary caregivers needing time to rest, work or attend appointments.
  • Families needing evening or weekend childcare support.
  • Families needing support during school holidays and breaks.
  • Children who benefit from individualized attention and structured activities.
  • Children requiring additional developmental, social or physical support where Tender Care is appropriately equipped and qualified.
  • Families looking for dependable, compassionate childcare support.
Individualized support

We begin with the child.

Care planning considers the child’s interests, routines, strengths, abilities, communication, comfort, developmental needs and family expectations.

Parents and caregivers remain important partners in creating a consistent and meaningful experience.

How care begins

A thoughtful start from first conversation to ongoing support.

Connect

Talk with Tender Care about the support you are seeking.

Understand

Share routines, interests, strengths and important considerations.

Plan

Agree on schedule, activities and family expectations.

Support

Care is provided with compassion, dignity and attentiveness.

Communicate

Families receive relevant updates about the child’s experience.

Activities

Meaningful, age-appropriate experiences.

Depending on the care plan, activities may include arts and crafts, sensory play, outdoor activity, reading, music, structured or unstructured play and agreed community activities.

Family communication

Clear information builds trust.

Tender Care values regular communication with parents and caregivers about activities, experiences and important information relevant to care.
