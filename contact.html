Make the first conversation useful

A few details can help Tender Care understand what support you are looking for.

You do not need to have every answer ready. The goal is simply to begin with enough information to discuss suitability, expectations and next steps.

Type of support

Respite, after-school, evening, weekend, holiday or other requested support.

Preferred schedule

Days, times and expected duration of care.

Child information

Age, routines, interests and relevant support needs.

Important considerations

Allergies, dietary needs, safety information or specialized support requests.

Prepare before you call

The Family Intake form can help organize the important details.

It includes space for routines, interests, comfort items, emergency contact information, allergies, dietary needs, important developmental or communication considerations and the family’s goals for respite care.
